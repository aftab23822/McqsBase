### What subjects are commonly included in teaching jobs tests in Pakistan?

The pattern varies by recruiting department and post, but teacher screening tests may include pedagogy, educational psychology, English, Pakistan Studies, Islamiat, general knowledge, science and basic mathematics.

### Are these questions copied from an official past paper?

No. These are original practice questions written in an exam-style format. They are intended to help with revision and are not presented as questions from an official or exact past paper.

### How should I use these teaching test MCQs?

Attempt the set under timed conditions, check your answers, and revise the underlying concepts for questions you missed. Then compare your preparation with the syllabus in the relevant recruitment notice.

### Do all teacher recruitment tests in Pakistan follow the same syllabus?

No. Syllabi and question weightings can differ among provincial commissions, testing services, departments and teaching posts. Always check the current official advertisement or syllabus.
