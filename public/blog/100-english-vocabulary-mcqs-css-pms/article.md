## A hundred words, one honest warning

That is exactly why this guide uses active [MCQ practice](https://www.mcqsbase.com/mcqs) rather than passive word collecting.

## Five vocabulary traps that cost easy marks

| Trap | What goes wrong | Better response |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sound-alike guessing | A familiar sound is mistaken for a familiar meaning. | Check roots and context; do not reward a word for sounding educated. |
| Part-of-speech mismatch | A noun is chosen as the synonym of an adjective. | Compare grammatical role before meaning. |
| Near-synonym confusion | A related word is selected although the shade of meaning differs. | Choose the closest meaning, not merely the same topic. |
| Missing the instruction | A synonym is selected in an antonym question. | Circle "same" or "opposite" before reading options. |
| One-and-done revision | A word is recognised once and then forgotten. | Retest after 1, 3 and 7 days. |

## Frequently asked questions

Are synonyms and antonyms included in the CSS syllabus

Yes. The official FPSC English (Precis & Composition) syllabus specifically lists synonyms and antonyms under Grammar and Vocabulary and also includes grouping words by similar or opposite meaning.

Are these exact CSS or PMS past-paper questions

No. They are fresh exam-level practice questions based on the vocabulary skills officially tested. The article does not mislabel original questions as past-paper items.

How many vocabulary words should I learn daily for CSS or PMS

For most learners, 10-20 words with active recall, examples and spaced revision is more useful than memorising a much larger list once.

Should I study synonyms or antonyms first

Study the core meaning first. Then learn one strong synonym, one strong antonym and one sentence. This creates a small network around the word.

Is reading a newspaper enough for vocabulary preparation

Reading helps you meet words in context, but it is not enough by itself. Add a word log, MCQs, sentence use and weekly retesting.

How can I remember difficult CSS vocabulary

Use retrieval practice: cover the meaning, recall it, write a sentence, and test it again after 1, 3 and 7 days. Roots and prefixes also help.

What is the best way to use this 100-MCQ set

Attempt it in four blocks of 25, analyse every mistake, then retake all 100 under a timer after one week.

Can these English MCQs help with essays and precis writing

Yes, when you go beyond recognition. Use selected words naturally in sentences and written practice; avoid forcing advanced vocabulary where a simple word is clearer.
