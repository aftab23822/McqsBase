## How to revise this Pakistan Geography and Map Skills set

1. For river questions, sketch the direction of flow and the order in which tributaries meet; do not assume nearby rivers join at the same place.
2. Make a simple outline map and label the mountain ranges, deserts, plateaus and passes in their broad regions before memorising names.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are these official Pakistan geography questions or past-paper questions?

No. They are original independent practice questions and are not presented as official syllabus material or copied from a past paper.

### What is a reliable way to solve map-scale questions?

Set up a proportion using the scale exactly as stated, calculate the ground distance in the same units, and then convert units if needed. For example, a scale of 1 cm to 5 km means 3 cm represents 15 km.

### How should I practise bearings?

Draw a north arrow at the starting point, measure bearings clockwise from north, and mark each leg of the route in order. This helps prevent confusing a direction with its reverse.

### Do these questions cover every exam’s geography syllabus?
