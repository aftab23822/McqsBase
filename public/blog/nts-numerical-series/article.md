## How to revise this NTS-Style Numerical Series and Number Patterns set

1. Write the differences between adjacent terms before guessing a rule; if they are not constant, compare the differences themselves.
2. For a sequence that appears irregular, split it into odd- and even-position terms and inspect each subsequence.
3. Test a proposed operation on every transition. A rule that explains only the last two terms may be a coincidence.
4. For multiplication-plus-addition patterns, state both operations in order and apply them to the final term.
5. Keep calculations exact until the final step, especially when a sequence includes division or decimals.
6. Review missed questions by the kind of rule you overlooked, not only by memorising the answer.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a numerical series question testing?

It tests whether you can infer a consistent relationship between the terms and apply it to an unknown term. Common relationships include differences, ratios, alternating operations and patterns in the gaps.

### What should I check if a sequence does not have a constant difference?

Calculate consecutive differences and inspect their pattern. If that does not help, check ratios, alternating operations, or the odd- and even-position terms separately.

### Can a numerical sequence have more than one possible rule?

A short list can sometimes be extended in many mathematical ways. In aptitude questions, look for the simplest consistent rule that explains all displayed terms and fits one option.
