## Test focus topics

| No. | Focus Area | What to Practice |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Main idea and supporting details | Separate a passage’s central point from examples, minor details and tempting but unsupported options. |
| 2 | Inference and implication | Draw conclusions that follow from the passage without adding assumptions the writer did not state. |
| 3 | Vocabulary in context | Use surrounding words and contrasts to determine a word’s meaning in that particular passage. |
| 4 | Reference and passage structure | Identify what pronouns refer to and how examples, contrasts and cause-and-effect links support a passage. |
| 5 | Claims, evidence and tone | Distinguish evidence from opinion and recognize cautious, critical or balanced language. |

## How to revise this NTS-Style Reading Comprehension Practice set

1. For missed main-idea questions, summarize the whole passage in one sentence before reviewing the options.
2. For inference questions, underline the exact phrase that supports your conclusion and reject choices that require outside assumptions.
3. When a word’s meaning is unfamiliar, read the whole sentence and look for examples, contrasts or cause-and-effect clues.
4. For pronoun-reference questions, substitute each possible noun into the sentence and check which one preserves the meaning.
5. On evidence questions, separate what the passage observes from what it concludes; a limited example does not prove a universal claim.
6. Keep an error log by skill type so you can tell whether a mistake came from misreading, vocabulary or over-interpreting.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are these questions official NTS questions?

No. They are original, independent practice questions written in an aptitude-test style, not questions copied from an official paper.

### What should I do when an inference seems possible but is not directly stated?

Choose it only if the passage gives enough evidence to support it. A plausible idea that depends on outside knowledge is not a sound text-based inference.

### How should I approach vocabulary questions in this set?

Reread the sentence and its nearby context, then test whether a proposed meaning fits the passage’s contrast, example or outcome.

### Does this set confirm the current format of an NTS test?

No. It practises reading comprehension only and does not establish the sections or arrangements of a particular test. Check the latest official notice for those details.
