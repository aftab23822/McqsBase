## How to revise this NAT-IE Chemistry: Atomic Structure and Chemical Bonding set

1. For isotope and ion questions, keep proton count, neutron count and electron count separate; an ion's charge changes electrons, not protons.
2. When writing ionic formulas, balance total positive and negative charge, then reduce subscripts to the lowest whole-number ratio.
3. For periodic trends, compare positions and electron-shell structure rather than memorizing isolated element pairs.
4. Draw a Lewis structure before deciding a molecule's shape; count lone pairs as electron domains even though they are not atoms.
5. For multiple bonds, remember that each bond contains one sigma bond, while a double bond adds one pi bond and a triple bond adds two.
6. Review intermolecular forces separately from intramolecular bonds: attractions between molecules help explain properties such as boiling point.
