## How to revise this COMSATS Entry Test Quantitative Aptitude Practice set

1. For fraction and decimal questions, write down the operation before calculating; convert to a common denominator when adding fractions.
2. In percentage problems, distinguish the original value from the changed value. For successive changes, apply each percentage to the current amount rather than adding the rates automatically.
3. Translate ratio statements into equal-sized parts before finding individual quantities.
4. For algebra questions, perform the same operation on both sides and substitute the result back into the original equation to check it.
5. In rate questions, keep units consistent. Convert minutes to hours or kilometres to metres before combining quantities.
6. Sketch a labelled diagram for geometry questions, and check that an area answer uses square units while a volume answer uses cubic units.
7. When reviewing a wrong answer, identify whether the error was a misunderstood rule, a calculation slip, or a unit conversion.
