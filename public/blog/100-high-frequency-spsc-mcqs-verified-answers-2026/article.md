### What "High-Frequency" Means Here

Official SPSC pages publish syllabi, selected past papers and answer keys, but they do not provide a single public database proving how many times every MCQ has appeared. So this article does not invent a repetition percentage. The set combines three defensible layers: reported SPSC paper questions, concepts visible across SPSC preparation material, and stable syllabus-aligned facts verified through authoritative references.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Are these 100 MCQs officially issued by SPSC

No. This is an independently prepared practice set. It uses reported SPSC past-paper questions where identifiable and fills the remaining coverage with syllabus-aligned, fact-checked MCQs. Official papers, syllabi and answer keys remain the final reference.

Why does the title say "high-frequency" instead of "most repeated"

Because a public official count of exact repeats is not available for every question. High-frequency describes recurring subject areas and commonly tested concepts without inventing a repetition number.

Where can I find official SPSC answer keys

Use the Answer Keys section of the official SPSC website and match the post, test date and paper type carefully.

Is this set useful for every SPSC post

It is useful for broad general preparation, but each post can have a different subject distribution. Always download the official scope and syllabus for the exact advertisement and post.

How many questions should I practise daily

Twenty carefully reviewed questions are more useful than 100 rushed guesses. Increase volume only when you are consistently checking explanations and revising mistakes.

Should I memorise all 100 answers

Memorise essential facts, but understand the concept and context. Question wording changes; understanding travels better than a memorised option letter.

How should I revise wrong answers

Maintain an error log with the topic, your wrong choice, the correct reason and a review date. Reattempt after one day, three days and seven days.

Can I use MCQsBase for further SPSC preparation

Yes. Use its topic-wise categories, quizzes, past-paper section and preparation articles as a structured daily practice hub.
