## Frequently Asked Questions

What is Everyday Science?

Everyday Science is basic scientific knowledge connected to ordinary life, including the human body, biology, physics, chemistry, the environment, Earth science and space.

Which topics are included in Everyday Science MCQs?

Common areas include human-body facts, plants and cells, units and measurements, light and sound, basic chemistry, atmosphere, Earth structure, energy and the solar system.

Are these 100 MCQs suitable for general knowledge learning?

Yes. The set is intentionally broad and foundational, making it useful for general knowledge revision as well as mixed MCQ-based screening and recruitment tests.

How should I practice these questions?

Try 20 at a time without checking the answers, score yourself, note the misses, and revisit only the weak facts after a short gap.
