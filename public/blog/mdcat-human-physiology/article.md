| 2 | Which part of a neuron usually receives signals from other neurons?<br />A) Dendrites<br />B) Myelin sheath<br />C) Axon terminal<br />D) Node of Ranvier | **A) Dendrites**<br />Dendrites commonly receive incoming signals and carry them toward the cell body. | 4 | What is the main role of myelin around many axons?<br />A) To increase the speed of impulse conduction<br />B) To release neurotransmitters into the blood<br />C) To produce the neuron's DNA<br />D) To detect changes in blood glucose | **A) To increase the speed of impulse conduction**<br />Myelin insulates the axon, allowing impulses to travel rapidly by jumping between nodes of Ranvier. | 5 | At a chemical synapse, what is released from the presynaptic terminal to pass a signal across the synaptic cleft?<br />A) Neurotransmitter<br />B) Myelin<br />C) Haemoglobin<br />D) Bile | **A) Neurotransmitter**<br />An arriving impulse triggers release of neurotransmitter molecules into the synaptic cleft. | 6 | Which sequence correctly represents a basic reflex arc?<br />A) Receptor, sensory neuron, integration centre, motor neuron, effector<br />B) Effector, sensory neuron, receptor, motor neuron, integration centre<br />C) Motor neuron, receptor, effector, sensory neuron, integration centre<br />D) Receptor, motor neuron, sensory neuron, effector, integration centre | **A) Receptor, sensory neuron, integration centre, motor neuron, effector**<br />A receptor detects a stimulus; a sensory neuron carries the signal to an integration centre, which directs a motor neuron to an effector. | 7 | Which structure is primarily responsible for coordinating balance and fine control of movement?<br />A) Cerebellum<br />B) Medulla oblongata<br />C) Hypothalamus<br />D) Spinal cord | **A) Cerebellum**<br />The cerebellum helps coordinate movement and balance. | 10 | Which response is generally associated with parasympathetic activity?<br />A) Increased digestive activity<br />B) Pupil dilation during acute stress<br />C) Increased heart rate during a threat<br />D) Reduced salivation during exertion | **A) Increased digestive activity**<br />Parasympathetic activity supports digestion and other rest-and-digest functions. Acute stress responses are more closely associated with sympathetic activity. |
| 11 | Which part of the eye changes its diameter to regulate how much light enters?<br />A) Iris<br />B) Cornea<br />C) Retina<br />D) Optic nerve | **A) Iris**<br />The iris adjusts pupil diameter and therefore controls light entry. The retina contains photoreceptors that detect light. |
| 12 | Which photoreceptors are especially important for colour vision in bright light?<br />A) Cones<br />B) Rods<br />C) Bipolar cells<br />D) Ganglion cells | **A) Cones**<br />Cones provide colour vision and high visual acuity in brighter conditions. | 13 | Which structure in the inner ear contains receptors for detecting head rotation?<br />A) Semicircular canals<br />B) Cochlea<br />C) Auditory tube<br />D) Eardrum | **A) Semicircular canals**<br />The semicircular canals detect rotational movements of the head. The cochlea is primarily involved in hearing. |
| 14 | Which hormone lowers blood glucose by promoting its uptake and storage?<br />A) Insulin<br />B) Glucagon<br />C) Adrenaline<br />D) Thyroxine | **A) Insulin**<br />Insulin promotes glucose uptake by many cells and supports storage of glucose as glycogen, lowering blood glucose. Glucagon generally raises it. |
| 15 | Which hormone is released by pancreatic alpha cells and tends to raise blood glucose?<br />A) Glucagon<br />B) Insulin<br />C) Calcitonin<br />D) Melatonin | **A) Glucagon**<br />Pancreatic alpha cells release glucagon, which promotes processes such as glycogen breakdown in the liver. Insulin is secreted by beta cells and generally lowers blood glucose. |
| 16 | Which gland produces thyroxine?<br />A) Thyroid gland<br />B) Adrenal cortex<br />C) Pancreas<br />D) Pineal gland | **A) Thyroid gland**<br />The thyroid gland produces thyroxine, which contributes to regulation of metabolic activity. The adrenal cortex produces steroid hormones, not thyroxine. |
| 17 | Which pituitary hormone stimulates the thyroid gland to release thyroid hormones?<br />A) Thyroid-stimulating hormone<br />B) Antidiuretic hormone<br />C) Oxytocin<br />D) Prolactin | **A) Thyroid-stimulating hormone**<br />Thyroid-stimulating hormone, or TSH, acts on the thyroid and promotes thyroid hormone release. | 18 | Which hormone helps the kidneys conserve water by increasing water reabsorption in collecting ducts?<br />A) Antidiuretic hormone<br />B) Insulin<br />C) Glucagon<br />D) Calcitonin | **A) Antidiuretic hormone**<br />Antidiuretic hormone, also called vasopressin, increases water reabsorption in the kidney collecting ducts. | 19 | Which hormone is released from the adrenal medulla during an acute stress response?<br />A) Adrenaline<br />B) Aldosterone<br />C) Cortisol<br />D) Thyroxine | **A) Adrenaline**<br />The adrenal medulla releases adrenaline, which helps prepare the body for rapid action. | 20 | Which hormone from the parathyroid glands raises blood calcium concentration?<br />A) Parathyroid hormone<br />B) Calcitonin<br />C) Insulin<br />D) Thymosin | **A) Parathyroid hormone**<br />Parathyroid hormone raises blood calcium through effects on bone, kidneys, and vitamin D activation.
