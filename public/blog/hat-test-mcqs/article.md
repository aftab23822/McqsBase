## How to revise this HAT Test Preparation set

1. Attempt the questions without checking the answers first, then review every incorrect or uncertain response.
2. For quantitative questions, write down the relevant formula or relationship before calculating.
3. Use elimination in verbal questions: remove choices that do not fit the sentence meaning or grammatical structure.
4. Translate analytical statements into a short sequence, table, or set of conditions before drawing a conclusion.
5. Practise with a timer, but prioritise accuracy while learning new question types.
6. Keep a short error log and revisit recurring weak areas during your next study session.
