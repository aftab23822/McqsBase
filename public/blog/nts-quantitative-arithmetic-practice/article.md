## How to revise this NTS-Style Quantitative Arithmetic Aptitude set

1. For percentage-change questions, identify the original value first; a rise and a later fall may use different bases.
2. In ratio questions, add the ratio parts before finding the value of one part.
3. For averages, convert the average back to a total before combining or removing group members.
4. Write distance, speed, and time in compatible units before calculating; convert minutes to hours where needed.
5. For combined work, add work rates rather than the workers' completion times.
6. In profit and discount problems, calculate each percentage from its stated base, usually cost price or marked price.
7. Review wrong answers by naming the specific operation or formula you misapplied, then solve the item again without looking.
