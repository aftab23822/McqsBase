Verbal Analogy Practice MCQs Practice Set for November 15, 2026

This independent practice set develops the skill of identifying the precise relationship between two words and applying it to another pair. It moves from familiar links—such as tool and function or part and whole—to more abstract relationships, including cause and effect, degree, sequence, and classification. For each question, state the relationship in your own words before comparing the options; this helps avoid choosing an answer based only on a loose association.
