## Frequently Asked Questions

### Are these questions taken from an official ISSB paper?

No. They are original, independent practice questions and are not presented as official, past-paper or confirmed-format questions.

### What should I do if two patterns seem possible?

Prefer the simplest rule that explains every item in the sequence or grid. Check each feature systematically and reject a rule that fails on even one given figure.

### How can I practise rotation questions accurately?

Choose a distinctive corner, mark or arm of the figure, then follow its position through the stated turn. A reflection reverses orientation; it does not simply turn the figure.

### Should I rely on this set for current test arrangements?

No. This set teaches general visual-reasoning skills. Test arrangements can vary, so consult the latest official notice for current information.
