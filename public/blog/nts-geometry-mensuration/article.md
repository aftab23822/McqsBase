## How to revise this NTS-Style Geometry and Mensuration set

1. For each missed item, name the quantity being asked for before choosing a formula: perimeter, area, volume, or surface area.
2. Write units in every measurement step. Area uses square units and volume uses cubic units.
3. Review angle rules separately: complementary angles total 90°, supplementary angles total 180°, and vertical angles are equal.
4. For circle questions, identify whether the given measurement is a radius or diameter before substituting into a formula.
5. On coordinate questions, keep the order of subtraction consistent in the slope formula and square both coordinate differences for distance.
6. Redo missed questions without looking at the explanation, then check whether the error was a formula choice, arithmetic step, or unit conversion.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does this set represent the official NTS syllabus?

No. It is independent aptitude-style practice on geometry and mensuration. Test content and arrangements can vary, so consult the latest official notice for the test you plan to take.

### Which circle value should I use for pi?

Use the value stated in a question when one is supplied. If none is specified, leave answers in terms of π when the options allow it, or use the stated approximation if the problem provides one.

### How can I avoid confusing surface area and volume?

Surface area measures the outside covering of a solid and is expressed in square units. Volume measures the space it contains and is expressed in cubic units.

Try the questions first without reading the explanations. Mark uncertain answers, then review the relevant rule and solve those questions again from scratch.
