### Why does COUNT(column) sometimes give a different result from COUNT(*)?

COUNT(*) counts rows, while COUNT(column) counts only rows where that column is not NULL. Check which measure a question asks for.

Identify the table's key and its dependencies, then ask whether attributes depend on the whole key and nothing but the key. Use the explanation to classify the design issue.
